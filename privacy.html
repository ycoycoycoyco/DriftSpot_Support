Privacy Policy

Drift Spot · Last updated November 5, 2026

Drift Spot is a casual driving game. This page explains what data is involved when you play and who handles it.

No accounts, no personal data from you

Drift Spot has no sign up and no login. It does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Stored on your iPhone

Your progress (levels, stars, cars, fuel), your stats in the Trophy Room and your settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. The developer does not run any servers and does not receive this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch a video ad to refuel, the ad is provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising and vendor identifiers, IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad.

  • Drift Spot does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so Google does not receive your advertising identifier (IDFA) through Drift Spot.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

If you buy Unlimited Runs, no more ads are requested.

Purchases

Unlimited Runs is bought through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Drift Spot never sees your payment details.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the achievements you earn are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear in your Game Center profile. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Drift Spot does not receive your Apple ID details.

Sharing

If you tap Invite a friend, iOS opens its share sheet and you choose where the message goes. Drift Spot does not see who you send it to.

Children

Drift Spot is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct or delete. For data handled by Google for advertising, use the privacy choices in the app or Google's tools linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
